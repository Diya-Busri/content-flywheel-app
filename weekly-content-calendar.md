# Content Flywheel — Weekly Content Calendar
### Faceless | Repeatable | One recording = a week of content

---

## NON-NEGOTIABLES (Every Day)
1. 2 job applications
2. One outreach action (Reddit / X / TikTok / Skool)
3. Reply to every comment and DM

---

## MONDAY — Pain Point Day
**Theme:** Speak to your audience's struggles

- **X:** Text post — a pain point your audience has
  > *"The hardest part of selling digital products isn't making them. It's figuring out what to make in the first place."*
- **Reddit:** Comment on 2-3 posts helpfully. No promotion yet.
- **TikTok:** Repurpose last week's best clip or skip if nothing ready

---

## TUESDAY — Build in Public Day
**Theme:** Show what you're working on

- **X:** Screenshot or screen record of something you built/fixed
  > *"Fixed [X] today. Here's what it looks like now vs before."*
- **Reddit:** Post or comment where relevant (once karma is built up)
- **TikTok:** 30-sec screen record — show the product doing something cool. No face. Just screen + text overlay.

---

## WEDNESDAY — YouTube Day ⭐
**Theme:** Full walkthrough — the core content piece everything else comes from

- **YouTube:** Full screen record walkthrough
  - "How I built a digital product in 10 minutes with AI"
  - "How to create your first ebook/planner/template using Content Flywheel"
  - No face needed. Screen record + voiceover or text captions.
- **X:** Post the YouTube link with a one-liner hook
- **TikTok:** Cut the best 30-60 seconds from the YouTube video and post it

---

## THURSDAY — Repurpose Day
**Theme:** Get more from Wednesday's content

- **TikTok:** Second clip from Wednesday's YouTube (different angle/moment)
- **X:** Pull a quote or insight from the video as a text post
- **Reddit:** If relevant, share the YouTube link in a thread where someone asked a related question
- **Skool:** Share the walkthrough as a resource

---

## FRIDAY — Results / Social Proof Day
**Theme:** Show traction, even small wins

- **X:** Weekly wrap-up post
  > *"This week: X job apps sent. X Reddit comments. X people visited the site. Still at 0 paying users. Shipping anyway."*
- **TikTok:** Before/after screen record — blank page → finished digital product using the tool
- **Reddit:** Check all your posts for new comments. Reply to everything.

---

## SATURDAY — Content Batch Day
**Theme:** Prepare next week so you don't think on the day

- Write next week's X posts (Mon/Tue/Fri) in one sitting
- Find 3 Reddit threads to comment on next week
- Plan Wednesday's YouTube topic
- Schedule anything that can be scheduled

---

## SUNDAY — Off or Void Hour Content
**Theme:** Low effort, high authenticity

- **X:** One casual post — thought, observation, or question
- **Void hour content:** Whatever format this is — keep it consistent and low effort
- Rest. You're running a business and job hunting at the same time.

---

## CONTENT BANK — Ideas to Pull From Anytime

**Pain points (X / Reddit posts):**
- "Nobody talks about how hard it is to name your first digital product"
- "The #1 reason beginners never launch their digital product"
- "What to sell online if you have no idea what to make"
- "Why your Etsy shop has no sales (it's not what you think)"

**Build in public (X posts):**
- "Day X: still 0 users. Here's what I changed today."
- "Someone said [harsh feedback]. They were right. Here's what I'm fixing."
- "Hit [small milestone]. Proof that showing up daily compounds."

**TikTok screen record ideas:**
- "I made a digital planner in 10 minutes" (screen record of the tool)
- "From idea to product in one sitting" (time-lapse style)
- "This is what Content Flywheel actually does" (demo)
- "The digital product I'd sell if I was starting from scratch" (using the tool live)

---

## THE FLYWHEEL IN PRACTICE

```
Wednesday YouTube walkthrough
        ↓
Thursday TikTok clip + X quote
        ↓
Friday results post drives curiosity
        ↓
Monday pain point post attracts new audience
        ↓
Tuesday build post builds trust
        ↓
Repeat → someone clicks the link → first paying user
```
